# Third-party data

## Open Food Facts snack catalog

`app/src/main/assets/indian_snacks_100.json` is a 100-product extract of the
Open Food Facts database, retrieved on 2 October 2026. Attribution: **Open Food
Facts contributors**. Per-product source URLs and preparation notes remain in
the file. The public JSON provides the extracted data in a reusable format.

Retain attribution and observe the database license when redistributing or
adapting this extract. The project's MIT source-code license does not replace
these data licenses.

The catalog contains links to Open Food Facts nutrition images; image files
are not bundled. Those images have their own CC BY-SA terms and may include
third-party packaging rights. See [Open Food Facts reuse documentation](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/).

Community records are not manufacturer-verified; the app asks users to check
their packet before logging and does not invent missing nutrition values.
